SyncUp

An agent that understands your company and brings verified people with real proof of work, not AI-optimized PDFs.

The Insight

Everyone is building AI to screen faster.
That's the wrong problem.

Faster screening of fake applications is just faster noise. The only way out isn't a better filter. It's changing what gets screened: verified proof instead of self-reported claims, and an agent that does the work on top of it.

The whole market is optimizing the wrong variable.
The Problem

Gartner says 1 in 4 job applications will be faked using AI by 2028.

100% of them will be optimized to match the exact job description.

Recruiters can't tell who's real

Every application reads as equally qualified, because it was written to match the job description word for word.

No verified identity

A resume or LinkedIn profile confirms nothing. Anyone can claim any degree, any role, any skill.

The cost lands on both sides

Recruiters lose hours screening inflated resumes and chasing candidates who don't qualify. Job seekers send dozens of applications before a single real response.

The system rewards a good resume, not real work.

The Solution

SyncUp is an agent that does the hiring work
and gets smarter with every hire.

For companies, an agent that sources, screens, and schedules. For job seekers, a verified profile that gets them found. Each side makes the other stronger.

The Moat

This isn't a feature they can add.
It's a model they can't switch to.

LinkedIn & Naukri

Built on self-reported profiles and ad/listing revenue. Verification would cannibalize the model they monetize.

Juicebox, Fabric, Weekday

Built on scraped and aggregated data. You can't verify what you scraped without consent. Their data advantage is their verification ceiling.

SyncUp

Verification is the foundation, not a layer. Every hire adds to a network of who verified whom that no competitor can copy by launching tomorrow.

Data is not the moat. Everyone rents the same profiles.
The accumulated network of verified relationships is.
